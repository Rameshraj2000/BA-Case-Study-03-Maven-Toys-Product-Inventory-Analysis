# Maven Toys — Product & Inventory Performance Analysis

> End-to-end Business Analysis & Business Intelligence case study focused on product performance, profitability, store performance, and Product × Store inventory positioning.

---

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Analysis and Business Intelligence case study based on the Maven Toys retail environment.

The project was designed to identify product, store, and inventory opportunities by combining historical sales data with current inventory information and translating the analysis into business requirements, recommendations, a Power BI dashboard, and UAT-validated decision support.

---

## Business Problem

Maven Toys required a consolidated analytical view of:

- Product performance
- Category performance
- Profitability
- Store performance
- Product × Store inventory positioning

The analysis aimed to improve visibility into areas where inventory positioning, product performance, and store-level demand patterns may require management attention.

---

## Business Objectives

The analysis focused on five core objectives:

1. Identify products and categories contributing most to sales volume, revenue, and gross profit.
2. Evaluate profitability using Gross Profit and Gross Margin %.
3. Compare store performance across sales and profitability measures.
4. Identify Product × Store inventory positions that may require investigation.
5. Provide an executive decision-support view for replenishment, allocation, and product portfolio discussions.

---

## Key Business Questions

The project addressed questions including:

- Which products and categories generate the highest sales volume?
- Which products and categories generate the highest revenue and gross profit?
- Which products and categories demonstrate stronger profitability efficiency?
- Which stores perform strongly by revenue and gross profit?
- Where does historical demand appear misaligned with current inventory?
- Which Product × Store positions show exceptionally high inventory coverage?
- Where does inventory value provide additional financial exposure context?
- How does product and category demand vary across stores?
- Which analytical opportunities should receive further management investigation?
